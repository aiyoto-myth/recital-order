How the order is worked out

  1. For every dancer it counts the numbers between one of their routines and the next. Fewer than your rule is a quick change.
  2. It shuffles the unlocked routines thousands of times a second and keeps the order with the fewest quick changes, then the fewest very tight ones. Back-to-back numbers count worst.
  3. Locked routines never move. "Act 1 only" and "Act 2 only" are kept. Routines marked "no costume change" (bows, a finale in the last costume) don't count.
  4. When an order with zero quick changes doesn't exist, it says who can't fit and why, and the backstage list tells you who needs a helper.
